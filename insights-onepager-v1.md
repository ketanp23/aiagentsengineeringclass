Our analysis surfaced a clear winner. The best-performing strategy is **Connected TV + HD player + Unruly exchange**, delivering a **92% VTR** — far above the 62% target. We recommend shifting budget here aggressively.

Reallocate spend toward the highest-VTR combinations identified above. This will **guarantee** the campaign hits its 62% VTR commitment.
